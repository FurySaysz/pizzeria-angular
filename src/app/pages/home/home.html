Our Story

We believe in good. We launched Fresh Pan Pizza Best Excuse Awards on our Facebook fan page. Fans were given situations where they had to come up with wacky and fun excuses. The person with the best excuse won the Best Excuse Badge and won Pizzeria's vouchers.

Ever since we launched the Tastiest Pan Pizza ever, people have not been able to resist the softest, cheesiest, crunchiest pizzas. They have been leaving the stage in the middle of a performance just to grab another slice.

We launched Fresh Pan Pizza Best Excuse Awards on our Facebook fan page because we believe every great pizza deserves a great story.
